v2.2.0 MAJOR UPDATE
FROM v2.1.0
  • 01
    PERFORMANCE MODE — one switch in Settings turns off the sun's shadow map, renders at 0.8× resolution, pulls the draw distance and fog in to 140 m, and stops UI animation and blur. Aim, hit detection, loot, bots and building are untouched: it costs you looks, not an edge.
  • 02
    A SETTINGS TAB — performance mode and auto sprint now have their own tab instead of a chip row squeezed into the lobby. Each one is a switch, saves the moment you flick it, and shows what it is doing in the header.
  • 03
    MULTIPLAYER RESPECTS IT — joining a friend already dropped shadows for you. That drop and this switch now go through the same path, so turning one off never quietly puts the other back on.
